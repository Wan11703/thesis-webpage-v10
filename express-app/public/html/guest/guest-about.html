ABOUT US

Our Story

MedExtract was developed to address the growing need for intelligent systems capable of interpreting clinical prescriptions with precision and efficiency. By combining advanced image preprocessing techniques and natural language processing, our platform automates the extraction of vital medical data from handwritten or printed prescriptions.

At the core of our solution is BioBERT, a specialized model for biomedical named entity recognition, which identifies drug names with high accuracy. This information is then used to retrieve detailed and reliable drug-related data from DrugBank, supporting safer and more informed medical decisions.

Our Mission

Our mission is to harness the power of artificial intelligence and biomedical language models to simplify and enhance the clinical documentation process. Through the integration of BioBERT and trusted medical databases such as DrugBank, MedExtract provides healthcare professionals with timely, accurate, and comprehensive medication insights.

We are committed to supporting healthcare institutions by delivering innovative tools that improve data accessibility, streamline workflows, and ultimately contribute to better patient care outcomes.

OUR SOLUTION

MedExtract offers a seamless system that begins by scanning and preprocessing clinical prescriptions to ensure clarity and precision. Leveraging BioBERT, a domain-specific language model, our system performs named entity recognition to accurately identify drug names mentioned in the prescription.

Once identified, each drug name is cross-referenced with DrugBank to retrieve relevant medical data including dosage, indications, interactions, and side effects. This end-to-end automation ensures that healthcare professionals receive reliable and detailed insights without the need for manual extraction or cross-checking.
